# Problem Statement — CricBuzz Live Scoring

Design an in-memory **live cricket scoring system** (CricBuzz-style) for matches, ball-by-ball updates, commentary, and scoreboards.

## Scope

- Teams and players registry
- Schedule T20/ODI/TEST matches
- Toss and innings management
- Ball-by-ball scoring (runs, boundaries, extras, wickets)
- Live scoreboard with run rate and chase target
- Commentary feed per ball (Strategy)

## Out of scope

- Real-time push/WebSocket, cloud DB, player career stats DB
- DRS, super over, rain rules (DLS)
